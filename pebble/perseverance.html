In the quote, the writer wanted to tell that life is not just one big adventure, but a series of short adventures. These will make us a stronger better person in the long run. We can not let our fears guide us in life, we have to stand up and face it. If we always run from our fears and never face them; our fears will conquer us. We have to find ways to be strong, so when troubled times do come we will have the strength and courage to face and defeat them.
