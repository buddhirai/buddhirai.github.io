According to the writer of the quote, we should always know and value the things which are most important. We may have different kinds of work to be performed each day of our life but we should prioritize them. The most important work should be done before the others. We sometimes waste our time by doing less important things which may hinder the progress. This may result in unhappiness and regret. When we motivate and focus on ourselves to important work we will achieve success in life. Therefore, we should recognize the work which is most important.
